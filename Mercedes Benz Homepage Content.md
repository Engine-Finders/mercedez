**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q1: Is it worth replacing a Mercedes-Benz engine?**

It depends entirely on vehicle value versus replacement cost. A GLC or W206 C-Class will almost always justify replacement - the car is worth far more than the engine. A W203 C-Class often won't; replacement cost can exceed what the car is worth outright. Use our diagnostic calculator above for a personalised verdict.

**Q2: Which Mercedes-Benz engine should I avoid?**

No engine outright, but the OM651 carries a real, well-documented caveat: "Black Death" injector seal failure, worse on higher-mileage or poorly-maintained examples. It's a manageable, preventable issue with regular servicing - not a reason to avoid the engine entirely, but a reason to demand full history. The OM654 has the cleanest modern record.

**Q3: What is "Black Death" on a Mercedes diesel?**

Injector seal failure that lets combustion gases leak past a degraded copper washer, forming a thick carbon deposit around the injector base. Left untreated it can weld the injector into the cylinder head, turning a £400–£1,200 seal job into a major repair. Most common on the OM646 and OM651 engines. Caught early, it's straightforward and inexpensive.

**Q4: C-Class vs E-Class - which is more reliable?**

Both carry a ✅ Safe verdict in our data on their newer generations, but for different reasons. The C-Class W206 runs the more dependable OM654-only diesel range. The E-Class W213 benefits from a wider, well-supported engine range across OM654 and M276. Buy on generation and service history first, badge second.

**Q5: How do I know if my Mercedes-Benz is worth repairing?**

Compare your realistic vehicle value against the full replacement quote, not just the repair estimate. As a rule, if replacement cost sits above roughly 70% of what the car is worth, get a scrap valuation before committing - the same threshold that applies across our BMW site, reflecting premium German parts and labour costs. Use our Ownership Decision Tool for a personalised assessment.

**Q6: Which is the safest used Mercedes-Benz to buy?**

The GLC X254 carries the cleanest ✅ Safe verdict of any Mercedes-Benz in our data - strong values and a single, dependable diesel across the range. The W206 C-Class and W213 E-Class are close behind. See our full Mercedes-Benz Rankings for details.
