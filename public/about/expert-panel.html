Who We Are

The Mercedes Engine Expert Panel exists to ensure every piece of technical content on this site is accurate, honest and useful to owners.

We are not a garage, not a dealership and not affiliated with Mercedes‑Benz AG. We are an independent editorial resource that researches Mercedes engines, publishes what we find, and routes enquiries to a vetted network of 15+ specialist suppliers.

Our panel is the quality‑control layer between our research and publication. They review all engine knowledge centres, failure pages, data reports and cost guides. If a panel member disagrees with a conclusion, we go back to the data - and we only publish when the panel is satisfied.

This is the same panel that reviews our annual failure index and the methodology behind every [EM‑VERIFIED] figure you see on the site. Their work is documented in our corrections log, where we record any factual errors caught after publication and the panel’s recommended fix.

What They Review

Every piece of technical content on this site is reviewed by at least one panel member before publication.

  • Engine Knowledge Centres - technical specifications, failure summaries, cost data and reliability verdicts
  • Failure Pages - root cause mechanisms, affected VIN ranges, diagnostic sequences and cost tables
  • Data Reports - failure rates, mileage distributions, decision splits and methodology notes
  • Economics & Cost Guides - repair/replace decisions, threshold calculations and cost‑of‑ownership comparisons
  • Comparison Pages - technical accuracy of spec comparisons and winner declarations
  • Technical Reference - all torque specs, oil capacities, belt/chain configurations and engine code decoders

Panel members also review major updates to existing pages, especially when new failure patterns emerge or cost data changes significantly.

Editorial Independence

Our panel is structurally independent from our commercial activities.

We pay panel members a fixed fee for their time, regardless of what they conclude. They receive no commission, no referral fees, and no bonus based on how many readers click through to a quote. This is stated in our editorial standards and reinforced in every how‑it‑works page.

If a panel member identifies an error in a published article, we correct it and log the correction in our corrections log - no exceptions. This is not a theoretical policy; we have made corrections in the past and will continue to do so.

Our commercial relationship with suppliers (they pay for enquiries) does not influence panel decisions. The panel does not review suppliers; that is handled separately through our specialist vetting process, which is also published and independent.

How Accuracy Is Ensured

Beyond the panel review, we have a multi‑layer fact‑checking process.

  1. Data source verification - every number is tagged ([EM‑VERIFIED], [THIRD‑PARTY], etc.) and its provenance is documented in our methodology page.
  2. Cross‑reference with multiple sources - we triangulate proprietary enquiry data with manufacturer TSBs, real workshop feedback ([EM‑INTERVIEW]) and post‑sale surveys ([EM‑SURVEY]).
  3. Panel review - at least one panel member reads every final draft and signs off with a documented comment.
  4. Public corrections log - any error discovered after publication is logged, corrected, and re‑reviewed. The corrections log is publicly accessible.
  5. Annual methodology review - the panel reviews our data collection and analysis methods each year to ensure they remain sound. The latest review is documented in Engine Failure Index 2026.

This process is why our reliability ratings are cited by other sites and why our data pages are used as a reference by workshops and insurers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the experts on the panel?

They are independent Mercedes specialists - master technicians, engine rebuilders, data analysts and diagnostics experts - each with 15+ years of hands‑on Mercedes experience. We do not publish their names, but we disclose their roles and experience (see above).

Are panel members paid?

Yes, we pay them a fixed fee for their time reviewing content. They are not paid based on any commercial outcome (e.g., number of quotes generated) and they do not receive commissions from suppliers or workshops.

Do they work for Mercedes‑Benz?

No. Our panel members are independent of Mercedes‑Benz AG. They have experience working at Mercedes dealerships in the past but are not currently employed by the manufacturer.

How often do they review new content?

Every technical page is reviewed before publication. Major updates to existing pages are also reviewed. We also conduct a full annual review of all key data pages, which is noted in the Engine Failure Index.

Can I suggest a correction?

Yes. If you spot an error, use our contact form or the correction suggestion link on each page. We will investigate with the panel and, if confirmed, log the correction in our corrections log.

Does the panel endorse specific suppliers?

No. The panel does not recommend or endorse any particular workshop, supplier or brand. Their role is purely technical review of our content. Supplier recommendations are handled separately through our specialist vetting page.
