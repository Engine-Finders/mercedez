The Three-Step Process

Everything on this site is designed to get you from a worrying engine noise to a fixed car, with no guesswork.

1. Research

Start with our engine knowledge centres to understand your specific engine. If you have a symptom, browse our symptom hubs or failure pages to identify the likely issue and typical cost. All numbers are tagged - [EM‑VERIFIED] means real UK enquiry data.

2. Compare Quotes

Use our registration lookup or engine compatibility checker to tell us what you need. We route your enquiry to specialists who match your engine and location. They respond with fixed quotes - you compare, no pressure.

3. Book & Fix

Choose the quote that fits your budget and confidence level. The specialist books you in, carries out the work, and provides a written warranty on both the unit and the labour. We follow up to verify outcomes - see our verified outcomes page.

What Happens When You Submit an Enquiry

The process is straightforward, and we handle your data responsibly.

  1. You submit your registration or engine code via our registration lookup tool. We match your vehicle to the correct Mercedes engine family.
  2. We route your request to the most relevant specialists in our vetted network - typically 3 to 5, based on location, specialism and availability.
  3. Specialists respond directly with a fixed quote. There is no auction or bidding war; each specialist gives you their best price based on your specific needs.
  4. You compare the quotes, read the specialist's vetting profile, and choose who to proceed with.
  5. You book directly with the specialist. We are not involved in the transaction - you pay the workshop, not us.

We never share your data with anyone outside the vetted specialist network. Our data verification and corrections log pages explain how we handle information.

How We Choose the Specialists

We apply a published, seven-point vetting process before any specialist receives a single enquiry from us.

Every specialist in our network must demonstrate genuine Mercedes specialism, a verifiable trading history, a documented warranty on supply and fit, engine provenance (knowing where a reconditioned or used unit comes from), consistent response times, a documented complaint-handling process, and valid insurance and premises. We also apply four automatic disqualifiers - including no written warranty, quoting without diagnosis, unverifiable provenance, and a pattern of unresolved complaints.

We review specialists continuously, not just once. Response times, completion rates and post-job outcomes are tracked through follow-up surveys - the same data that powers our case studies and data reports. If a specialist's performance drops, they receive reduced routing; if they fail a disqualifying criterion, they are removed.

Full details are on our specialist vetting page.

What You Pay - and What You Don't

We are transparent about our commercial model, and we don't hide fees in your final bill.

You pay the specialist directly for the supply and fit of your replacement engine. The quote you receive is the price for the work - there are no additional "referral fees" or "booking charges" added by us. Our specialists are paid by us for the enquiry itself, not by you. That fixed fee does not depend on the size of the repair or whether you go ahead.

This model is why our editorial independence matters: if we only ever recommended the most expensive repair, we would generate more enquiries in the short term and destroy trust in the long term. That is why we publish our repair‑vs‑replace‑vs‑scrap framework - including the 70% Mercedes threshold - and why we have pages like when to scrap a Mercedes and is it worth replacing a C‑Class engine.

Our average quoted supply cost for a OM651 reconditioned unit is £2,800–£4,800 [EM‑QUOTE], and for a OM642 it is £3,200–£5,500 [EM‑QUOTE]. Labour is quoted separately by the specialist, typically at £65–£90 per hour depending on region.

Warranty & Aftercare

Every specialist in our network provides a documented warranty on both the supply and fit of your replacement engine.

The specific warranty period and terms vary by specialist and by whether you choose a used, reconditioned or rebuilt unit. However, as a minimum standard, we require a written warranty that covers both the engine itself and the labour to install it. This is one of our seven non‑negotiable vetting criteria.

Before you book, we recommend reviewing the specialist's warranty terms. Our warranty standards page explains what a good warranty should include, what to watch out for, and how to make a claim if something goes wrong.

If you have a dispute that cannot be resolved directly with the specialist, we can review the case and, if appropriate, log it in our public corrections log or route it through the independent adjudication process described in our editorial standards.

What We Don't Do

Our role is clearly defined. We are not a garage, not a dealership, and not a booking agent.

  • We do not fit engines. We are an editorial resource, not a workshop.
  • We do not hold stock. We don't buy, store, or sell engines.
  • We are not affiliated with Mercedes‑Benz AG. We are an independent site, and we state that plainly.
  • We do not charge you a booking fee. You pay the specialist directly - we take a fixed fee from the specialist for the enquiry, not from you.
  • We do not guarantee a specific quote. We route your enquiry; the specialist provides the price. Market conditions, vehicle condition and regional labour rates vary.

Our about page and expert panel page provide further detail on who we are and who reviews our work.

The Verdict - Why This Works

This model works because we are structurally independent and data‑driven.

We have no financial incentive to push you toward a replacement if a repair is viable, or toward a particular supplier. Our Engine Failure Index and most‑enquired models data are drawn from 9,000+ real UK enquiries [EM‑VERIFIED], not from manufacturer marketing or anonymous forum anecdotes.

When a specialist consistently delivers good outcomes, they continue to receive enquiries. When they don't, they are routed away. This accountability loop - combined with our published vetting criteria and verified outcomes - gives you a genuinely safer route than calling a random garage from a search result.

Our panel of independent Mercedes experts reviews every technical page, and our methodology is open. You can read it in full on our methodology page. If that changes, we log the change in our corrections log.
