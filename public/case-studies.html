Key Takeaways from Our Case Studies

Decision Patterns

Across all studies, the majority of owners chose a reconditioned engine over a used unit or a full rebuild - especially when a warranty was offered. The 70% scrap threshold is a real influence on older, lower‑value cars.

Satisfaction

Post‑sale satisfaction is consistently highest among those who chose a reconditioned engine with a comprehensive warranty. Used‑engine buyers report slightly lower satisfaction, mainly due to uncertainty about future reliability.

Cost Realities

The true cost of an engine replacement includes not just the supply cost but also labour, ancillaries, and potential follow‑up repairs. Our case studies break down these real numbers.

When to Scrap

For Mercedes models, the 70% threshold often tips the decision when the vehicle’s market value is below £4,000–£6,000. Our studies show exactly where that line is drawn for each model.

For a deeper dive into the decision framework, see our Repair, Replace, or Scrap Framework and the Data Methodology page.

How We Compile Case Studies

All case studies are based on real, anonymised data from our proprietary enquiry system and post‑sale surveys. We never invent a customer story.

  • Sample: Each study covers a minimum of 20–50 real cases, drawn from our 2025 CRM data.
  • Data sources: Enquiry descriptions, supplier quotes, post‑sale satisfaction surveys (3‑6 months after the work).
  • Anonymisation: All individual identifiers are removed. We report only aggregate patterns.
  • Validation: Data is cross‑checked with manufacturer TSBs, independent specialists, and market value guides.
  • Tagging: Every number is tagged as [EM‑VERIFIED], [EM‑SURVEY], or [THIRD‑PARTY] - see our data verification policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a case study, and how is it different from a blog post?

Our case studies are data‑driven analyses of real owner decisions, not opinion pieces. Each one is based on a defined sample of real enquiries, surveys, and interviews. We present the numbers, the patterns, and the lessons - without anecdotal storytelling.

Are these real customers? Can I see individual stories?

These are real customers in aggregate - we never publish named individual stories or quotes. All data is anonymised to protect privacy. We believe the pattern is more valuable than any one person’s experience.

How can I use these case studies to make my own decision?

Each case study shows what others in a similar position chose, what it cost, and how satisfied they were. You can compare your own vehicle’s value, mileage, and service history to the profiles in the study to gauge which route is most likely to work for you.

Where does the data come from?

Our primary sources are enquiry records from EnginesMarket.co.uk and EngineFinders.co.uk, plus voluntary customer surveys and specialist interviews. We also cross‑check with public data from AutoTrader, manufacturer TSBs, and trusted forums for validation.

Do you cover all Mercedes engines?

We currently have studies for the most‑requested engines: OM642, OM651, M272, M276, and AMG variants. We’re adding more as data accumulates. Check back regularly or subscribe to our newsletter for updates.
