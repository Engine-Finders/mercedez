Featured Comparison

Most-Read: OM651 vs OM642

The four-cylinder OM651 and the V6 OM642 are the two most-enquired Mercedes diesels in our data - 1,250 vs 880 enquiries in 2025 [EM-VERIFIED]. The OM651 is cheaper to replace (£2,800–£4,800 recon supply) and fitted across more models, but the OM642 offers more refinement and torque in larger vehicles like the E-Class, ML and GLE. The OM651's chain tensioner is a known risk; the OM642's oil cooler seal and swirl flap issues are well-documented. Both are safe to replace against typical host values, but the OM651 wins on cost-per-mile for most buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Mercedes diesel engine is most reliable, OM651 or OM642?

The OM651 is generally considered more reliable in terms of major failure frequency, though its timing chain tensioner is a known risk. The OM642 is more complex (V6) and has documented oil cooler and swirl flap issues, but many examples exceed 150,000 miles with proper maintenance. The OM654 is the most modern and generally the most robust, but fewer examples have reached high mileage yet.

Is a Mercedes OM651 engine worth replacing?

In most cases, yes - the OM651 is one of the most common and well-supported Mercedes diesels in the UK. A reconditioned OM651 costs around £2,800–£4,800 supply-only [EM-QUOTE], and most host vehicles (C-Class, E-Class, GLC) are worth well above that. Always apply the 70% threshold: if the fitted cost exceeds 70% of the vehicle's current market value, evaluate scrap options first.

What's the best Mercedes engine for long-term ownership?

For diesel, the OM654 (2016+) is the most refined and reliable modern unit. For petrol, the M276 V6 and M274 four-cylinder are well-regarded, with the M276 being more robust than the earlier M272. The OM642 V6 diesel is a strong choice if regularly maintained and driven on longer runs.

How do Mercedes engine costs compare across models?

Engine replacement costs vary significantly: the OM651 (C-Class/E-Class/GLC) is cheapest at £2,800–£4,800 supply-only. The OM642 V6 diesel runs £3,200–£5,500. The M276 V6 petrol is similar at £3,800–£6,500. AMG engines (M157, M178) are substantially more expensive, often exceeding £8,000 supply-only.
