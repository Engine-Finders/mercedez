Editorial Standards

We publish only editorial content that is independent, evidence‑based, and clearly signposted. Our editorial team is entirely separate from any commercial relationships. We do not accept payment for favourable coverage, and we do not allow manufacturers or suppliers to review content before publication.

  • Independence: No affiliate links, no sponsored posts, no paid reviews. All recommendations are based solely on data and specialist consensus.
  • Transparency: Every claim is sourced. Numbers are tagged with their provenance – [EM‑VERIFIED], [EM‑SURVEY], [THIRD‑PARTY], or [PATTERN DATA].
  • No AI‑generated content: All articles are written by human editors and reviewed by technical experts. We never publish AI‑generated text without full human oversight.
  • Plain language: We write for owners, not engineers. Technical terms are explained on first use.

For our full editorial charter, see Editorial Standards.

Specialist Vetting Process

We do not simply list any workshop that pays a fee. Every specialist in our network has passed a rigorous vetting process, conducted jointly by the Engine Finders team and independent automotive engineers.

Our checklist for vetting a Mercedes specialist:

  • ✅ Evidence of at least 5 years’ continuous trading in the UK
  • ✅ Dedicated Mercedes‑specific diagnostic equipment (Star Diagnosis, Xentry, etc.)
  • ✅ At least one technician with manufacturer‑level training (Mercedes‑Benz certified or equivalent)
  • ✅ Publicly available customer reviews with an average rating of ≥4.0 across at least 20 reviews
  • ✅ A clear written warranty policy for parts and labour (minimum 12 months)
  • ✅ Insurance and liability cover appropriate for the scale of work undertaken
  • ✅ Willingness to participate in our post‑sale customer satisfaction surveys

We also conduct mystery‑shop visits and review samples of recent invoices to ensure pricing transparency. Specialists who do not meet our standards are removed from the network. Read more about our vetting process →

Data Verification & Sourcing

Every piece of data on this site – from enquiry volumes to satisfaction rates – is sourced from one of four channels. We never invent a statistic or use an unverified number.

  • [EM‑VERIFIED] – Exact counts from our internal CRM (enquiry volumes, model requests, decision splits).
  • [EM‑SURVEY] – Post‑sale customer satisfaction surveys, conducted 3–6 months after repair.
  • [EM‑INTERVIEW] – Direct statements from mechanics, workshop owners, or supplier managers, aggregated and anonymised.
  • [THIRD‑PARTY] – External data from AutoTrader, Parkers, manufacturer TSBs, or other reputable sources, always cited.
  • [PATTERN DATA] – Expert‑knowledge patterns derived from multiple sources but not backed by a specific proprietary volume.

We periodically audit our data against independent sources and update our pages when new information becomes available. Our full methodology explains how we collect, clean, and interpret data.

Corrections Policy

We are committed to accuracy. If you spot an error – whether factual, statistical, or typographical – we want to know. Our corrections process is simple and transparent.

  • Report an error: Email us at corrections@mercedesengines.uk with the page URL and a clear description of the issue.
  • Review: Our editorial team will verify the claim against our source data within 5 working days.
  • Correction: If an error is confirmed, we will correct the page and note the change in our public corrections log.
  • Major corrections: For significant factual or data errors, we will also add a prominent notice on the affected page for 30 days.

We also regularly review our content and update it as new data becomes available – ensuring that what you read today is as accurate as possible.

Warranty Standards for Reconditioned Engines

When we recommend a reconditioned engine, we insist on a minimum warranty standard. This is non‑negotiable for our network.

Minimum requirements for any engine supplied through our network:

  • ✅ Minimum 12 months / 12,000 miles parts and labour warranty
  • ✅ Warranty is underwritten by a recognised insurance-backed scheme or directly by the supplier with proven financial backing
  • ✅ Clear written terms – no hidden exclusions for common wear items or consequential damage
  • ✅ Nationwide cover – the warranty is valid at any VAT‑registered garage in the UK, not just the supplying specialist
  • ✅ A clear, simple claims process with a dedicated helpline

Suppliers who cannot meet these standards are not part of our network. We also track warranty claim rates and use that data to refine our recommendations. Full warranty standards →
