Most Common Failure Modes (Ranked)

What actually fails on the M273, in order of frequency observed in our data.

1. Timing Chain Tensioner Wear

The M273's timing chain tensioner can wear, causing chain slack and a rattle on cold start. Unlike the M272, the M273 doesn't have the balance shaft sprocket issue, but tensioner wear is still a known weak point. A cold-start rattle lasting more than 2 seconds is the classic symptom [PATTERN DATA].

2. Coolant Crossover Pipe Leaks

The M273's coolant crossover pipes are prone to leaking, particularly on older examples. Coolant loss with no visible external leak is the typical symptom. Replacement can be labour-intensive due to V8 access, but the parts are reasonably priced [PATTERN DATA].

3. Oil Cooler Seal Leaks

The oil cooler seals can degrade, leading to oil leaks and in severe cases, oil contamination of the coolant. A well-documented issue on the M273 that requires the intake manifold to be removed for access. Regular oil checks and monitoring for oil in the coolant are essential [PATTERN DATA].

4. Intake Manifold Flap Failure

Similar to the M272, the M273's variable intake manifold has flaps that can fail, causing rough running and reduced power. Replacement manifold or flap repair is the standard fix [PATTERN DATA].

How Long Does an M273 Actually Last?

The data-driven answer on M273 lifespan.

Based on workshop feedback and pattern data [PATTERN DATA], the M273 typically reaches 120,000–180,000 miles before a major repair is required. This is an excellent lifespan for a naturally aspirated V8 - better than the M272 (100k–130k) and comparable to the later M276 (150,000+).

The M273's durability comes from its relatively simple design - it's a naturally aspirated, port-injected V8 without the complexity of direct injection or turbochargers. The failure points are well-understood and predictable: timing chain tensioner, coolant pipes, oil cooler seals. None of these are catastrophic if caught early [EM-INTERVIEW].

What separates a 120,000-mile M273 from a 180,000-mile one is almost always maintenance history. Engines with regular oil changes (every 7,000–10,000 miles), proactive coolant system work, and early attention to any timing chain rattle consistently outlast those where the first sign of trouble was a warning light. The M273 is a forgiving engine - it doesn't punish neglect immediately, but the bill will come eventually.

Our Verdict - The M273 Decision

Is an M273 failure a reason to scrap the car? The data says no.

The M273 is a ✅ Safe engine for repair - here's why.

With host vehicle values ranging from £8,000 to £35,000 [THIRD-PARTY] and a reconditioned supply cost of £2,500–£5,000 [EM-QUOTE], the replacement cost typically sits well below the 70% threshold that would trigger a scrap decision. Even at the upper end of the replacement cost (£5,000 + £1,170 labour for 18 hours at £65/hr), the total fitted cost of around £6,170 is still only 18–77% of the vehicle's value on a typical M273-powered S-Class or CLS.

The caveat: the M273's host vehicles are ageing. A W221 S-Class from 2006–2012 is now worth £5,000–£15,000 in average condition. A £6,000 repair on a £6,000 car (100% of value) is a scrap decision. But on a £20,000 CLS or low-mileage SL, it's a clear "go". The M273's reputation for durability means many owners are willing to invest in maintenance - and the data suggests that's a sound decision for higher-value examples.

For owners facing an M273 failure, the key is early diagnosis and choosing the right specialist. A timing chain tensioner rattle caught early is a £1,000–£2,000 repair. The same issue ignored until the chain jumps is an £6,000+ engine replacement. Regular inspections and addressing any cold-start rattle immediately are the two single most effective actions an owner can take.

Frequently Asked Questions

How reliable is the Mercedes M273 engine?

The M273 is one of the more durable naturally aspirated V8s Mercedes produced. It typically reaches 120,000–180,000 miles before needing major repair. Unlike the M272, it doesn't suffer from the balance shaft sprocket issue, making it a more reliable long-term proposition. The known failure points (timing chain tensioner, coolant crossover pipes, oil cooler seals) are well-understood and repairable.

What are the common signs of M273 failure?

Cold-start rattle lasting more than 2 seconds (timing chain tensioner), coolant loss with no visible external leak (crossover pipe), oil in the coolant or external oil leaks (oil cooler seals), and rough running (intake manifold flaps). Any of these should be investigated immediately.

How much does an M273 replacement cost?

A reconditioned M273 supply costs £2,500–£5,000 [EM-QUOTE]. Labour for removal and replacement is 14–18 hours at independent specialist rates (£65/hr), adding £910–£1,170. Total fitted cost ranges from £3,410 to £6,170. Main dealer costs would be significantly higher. Timing chain tensioner work, if caught early, is cheaper at £1,000–£2,000.

What's the difference between M273 and M278?

The M273 is the 5.5-litre naturally aspirated V8, used in W221 S-Class, C219 CLS, W211 E-Class and R230 SL from 2006–2011. The M278 is the 4.7-litre twin-turbo V8 that replaced it, with more power and better fuel economy but added turbo complexity. The M273 is simpler and often more durable, while the M278 is more modern and responsive but more expensive to maintain when turbos or direct injection components fail.

Should I buy a high-mileage M273?

Absolutely - the M273 is a robust engine and high-mileage examples (100,000+ miles) are viable if they have a full service history. Look for evidence of timing chain tensioner work and coolant pipe replacement - if these haven't been done, budget for them. A well-maintained M273 can easily reach 180,000 miles.

Is the M273 worth maintaining long-term?

Yes - the M273 is a durable, well-regarded V8 that holds up well with proper maintenance. The cost of major maintenance (timing chain, coolant pipes) is significant but still far less than depreciation on a newer car. For an S-Class, CLS or SL owner, investing in M273 maintenance is a sound financial decision if the rest of the car is in good condition.
