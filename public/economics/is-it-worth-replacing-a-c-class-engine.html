C‑Class Engine Replacement – By Generation

Each C‑Class generation has different economics. Here's how the W204, W205 and W206 stack up against the 70% threshold.

W204 (2007–2014) – ⚠️ Watch

With typical values of £4,000–£12,000, the W204 is the most marginal C‑Class. OM651 recon fitted costs £3,500–£5,600. On a £5,000 example, the repair is 70–112% – often a write‑off. On a £10,000 example, it's 35–56% – a safe repair. Know your car's value before deciding.

The Verdict – Is It Worth Replacing a C‑Class Engine?

The answer is overwhelmingly yes – with one important exception. Here's the honest verdict.

✅ Yes – W205 and W206 C‑Class

With values from £8,000 upwards, the repair cost is typically 20–50% of the car's value – well under the 70% threshold. The OM651 and OM654 are among the cheapest Mercedes engines to replace, and the W205's high enquiry volume (368 [EM-VERIFIED]) means parts and specialists are everywhere. Replacement is almost always the right decision.

⚠️ It Depends – W204 C‑Class

The W204 is the marginal generation. On a £10,000+ example, replacement is safe and sensible. On a £4,000–£6,000 example, the repair often exceeds the 70% threshold – making it a financial write‑off. Check your car's value before committing.

🔴 No – W204 Under £5,000

If your W204 C‑Class is worth less than £5,000, an engine replacement is almost never worth it – the repair cost will exceed 70% of the car's value. The honest advice is to sell for parts or scrap. See our scrapping guide for how to do this.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it worth replacing a C‑Class engine?

On most C‑Class models – yes. The W205 and W206 have strong values (£8,000–£45,000) and engine replacement costs from £3,500, making the repair well under the 70% threshold. Only low‑value W204s under £5,000 are usually not worth it.

How much does a C‑Class engine replacement cost in the UK?

Costs vary by engine: OM651 diesel from £2,800 supply (£3,500 fitted), OM654 diesel from £3,000 supply (£3,700 fitted), and M276 V6 petrol from £3,800 supply (£4,600 fitted). Labour adds £520–£1,040 depending on the engine.

Which C‑Class generation is cheapest to replace?

The W205 with OM651 is the cheapest to replace – recon fitted costs from £3,500. The W206 with OM654 is also very affordable. The W204 has similar costs but lower vehicle values, making it more marginal.

What is the 70% rule for C‑Class engine replacement?

The 70% rule: if the full fitted repair cost exceeds 70% of your C‑Class's current market value, the honest verdict is to scrap or sell for parts. For a W205 worth £9,500 with a £4,200 repair, the ratio is 44% – safe. For a W204 worth £4,500 with the same repair, it's 93% – scrap.

Is a W204 C‑Class worth repairing?

It depends. On a £10,000+ W204, yes – the repair is under 70% of the car's value. On a £4,000–£6,000 example, no – the repair exceeds 70%. Always check your car's current value before deciding.

Which C‑Class engine is most reliable?

The OM651 is generally reliable but has timing chain tensioner wear as a known weak point. The OM654 is more modern and generally more robust. Both are widely supported by specialists. See our OM651 and OM654 Knowledge Centres for full detail.
