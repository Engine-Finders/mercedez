The Verdict – Is It Worth Replacing a GLE Engine?

The answer is overwhelmingly yes – the GLE is one of the safest engine replacement decisions in the Mercedes SUV range. Here's the honest verdict.

✅ Yes – W166, V167 and C167 GLE

With values from £12,000 upwards, the repair cost is typically 10–40% of the car's value – well under the 70% threshold. The OM642 and OM654 are well‑supported engines with reasonable recon costs, and the GLE's high value bands make replacement a financially sensible decision on almost every example. Replacement is almost always the right decision.

🔴 The Only Exception – Very Low‑Value W166 Examples

If your W166 GLE is worth under £10,000 (unusual but possible on very high‑mileage early examples), a £6,400 repair could approach the 70% threshold. However, this is the exception – not the rule. On 95%+ of GLEs, the answer is a clear yes.

The GLE combines large‑SUV practicality, strong residual values and affordable engine replacement costs. It's a smarter long‑term ownership proposition than many rivals because the 70% threshold is rarely tested. Replacement is one of the easiest decisions you'll make.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it worth replacing a GLE engine?

Yes – on almost every GLE. The W166, V167 and C167 have strong values (£12,000–£65,000) and engine replacement costs from £4,000, making the repair just 6–40% of the car's value – well under the 70% threshold.

How much does a GLE engine replacement cost in the UK?

Costs vary by engine: OM642 diesel from £3,200 supply (£4,000 fitted), M276 V6 petrol from £3,800 supply (£4,600 fitted), and OM654 diesel from £3,000 supply (£3,700 fitted). Labour adds £650–£1,040 depending on the engine.

Which GLE generation is cheapest to replace?

The V167 with OM654 is the cheapest to source and has the highest values, making it the most financially sensible to replace. However, all generations are very affordable relative to their value.

What is the 70% rule for GLE engine replacement?

The 70% rule: if the full fitted repair cost exceeds 70% of your GLE's current market value, the honest verdict is to scrap or sell for parts. For a GLE worth £18,000 with a £5,200 repair, the ratio is 29% – safe. For a sub‑£10,000 example, it could approach 70% – but this is rare.

Is a GLE more expensive to replace than a GLC?

Yes – the GLE's OM642 V6 diesel is more expensive than the GLC's OM651 four‑cylinder, and labour hours are higher. However, the GLE's higher values mean the repair is still a smaller percentage of the car's value, making it an equally safe decision.

Which GLE engine is most reliable?

The OM642 is generally reliable but has swirl flap and oil cooler seal issues as known weak points. The OM654 is more modern and generally more robust. The M276 petrol is also well‑regarded. See our OM642, OM654 and M276 Knowledge Centres for full detail.
