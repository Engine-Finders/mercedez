Sprinter Engine Replacement – By Generation

Each Sprinter generation has different economics. Here's how the W901–W905, W906 and W907 stack up against the 70% threshold – and the commercial factor.

W901–W905 (1995–2006) – 🔴 Scrap

These older Sprinters are worth £1,500–£4,000. A £3,500 OM651 repair on a £2,500 van is 140% – a financial write‑off. Unless the van has exceptional commercial value or sentimental significance, replacement is not worth it.

The Verdict – Is It Worth Replacing a Sprinter Engine?

The answer depends on your Sprinter's age, value and whether it earns money. Here's the honest verdict.

✅ Yes – W907 Sprinter (2018+)

With values from £10,000 upwards, the repair cost is typically 14–40% of the van's value – well under the 70% threshold. The OM651 and OM654 are among the cheapest Mercedes engines to replace, and the W907's strong residual values make replacement a financially sensible decision. Replacement is almost always the right decision.

⚠️ It Depends – W906 Sprinter (2006–2018)

The W906 is the marginal generation. On a £7,500+ van, replacement is safe and sensible. On a £4,000–£6,000 van, the repair often approaches or exceeds the 70% threshold – but commercial use can justify it. If the van earns £200+ per week, a £4,200 repair pays for itself in months.

🔴 No – W901–W905 Sprinter (1995–2006)

If your Sprinter is pre‑2006 and worth under £4,000, an engine replacement is almost never worth it – the repair cost will exceed the van's value. The honest advice is to sell for parts or scrap. See our scrapping guide for how to do this.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it worth replacing a Sprinter engine?

On most modern Sprinters – yes. The W907 has strong values (£10,000–£25,000) and engine replacement costs from £3,500. The W906 is marginal – but commercial use can justify the repair. Only pre‑2006 W901–W905 Sprinters are usually not worth it.

How much does a Sprinter engine replacement cost in the UK?

Costs vary by engine: OM651 diesel from £2,800 supply (£3,500 fitted), and OM642 V6 diesel from £3,200 supply (£4,000 fitted). Labour adds £520–£910 depending on the engine.

Which Sprinter generation is cheapest to replace?

The W907 with OM651 is the cheapest to source and has the highest values, making it the most financially sensible to replace. The W906 has similar costs but lower values, making it more marginal.

What is the 70% rule for Sprinter engine replacement?

The 70% rule: if the full fitted repair cost exceeds 70% of your Sprinter's current market value, the honest verdict is to scrap or sell for parts. For a W906 worth £7,500 with a £4,200 repair, the ratio is 56% – safe. For a £3,500 van with the same repair, it's 120% – scrap.

Does commercial use change the 70% rule?

Yes – a working van that earns money can justify a higher repair cost. If the van pays for itself in under a year, the 70% rule becomes less relevant. We recommend calculating the payback period – if the repair cost is less than 12 months' earnings, it's usually worth it.

Which Sprinter engine is most reliable?

The OM651 is generally reliable but has timing chain tensioner wear as a known weak point. The OM642 is a V6 with swirl flap and oil cooler seal issues. Both are widely supported by specialists. See our OM651 and OM642 Knowledge Centres for full detail.
