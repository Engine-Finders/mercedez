OM651 - The Cheapest Mercedes Engine to Replace

The OM651 2.1‑litre 4‑cylinder diesel is the most‑enquired Mercedes engine in our 2025 data (1,250 requests [EM-VERIFIED]) – and the cheapest to replace.

Typical Applications

C‑Class (W204, W205), E‑Class (W211, W212), GLC (X253), GLA (X156), GLK (X204), A‑Class (W176), B‑Class (W246), Sprinter, Vito

Replacement Costs

Recon: £2,800–£4,800 supply [EM-QUOTE]
Used: £1,500–£2,800 supply [EM-QUOTE]
Fitted: £3,500–£5,600

Labour: 8–12 hours. The OM651 is widely available and supported – replacement is almost always financially viable on any Mercedes worth more than £5,000.

The Verdict – Which Engine Costs the Most?

The M177 AMG V8 is the most expensive standard Mercedes engine to replace, but the V12 M275/M279 is the costliest overall. Here's the summary.

💰 Cheapest: OM651

At £2,800–£4,800 supply, the OM651 is the cheapest Mercedes engine to replace. It's the most‑enquired engine for a reason – parts are everywhere, specialists know it inside out, and it's affordable to re‑engine.

🏆 Most Expensive (Non‑V12): M177

At £8,000–£14,000 supply, the M177 AMG V8 is the most expensive non‑V12 Mercedes engine to replace. On a £40,000+ AMG GT, it's still financially viable – but on a £20,000 C63, it's borderline.

🔴 Most Expensive Overall: V12 M275/M279

At £10,000–£20,000+ supply, V12 engines are the most expensive to replace. On a £10,000 S600, a £15,000 V12 replacement is 150% of the car's value – a clear write‑off. Only consider if your car is worth £30,000+.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Mercedes engine is cheapest to replace?

The OM651 2.1‑litre diesel is the cheapest – recon supply costs £2,800–£4,800. It's the most‑enquired engine in our data (1,250 requests in 2025) and parts are widely available.

How much does a Mercedes OM651 engine cost?

Supply‑only, a reconditioned OM651 costs £2,800–£4,800. Fitted, expect £3,500–£5,600. Used units are cheaper at £1,500–£2,800 but carry more risk.

Which Mercedes engine is most expensive to replace?

The V12 M275/M279 is the most expensive – recon supply costs £10,000–£20,000+. The M177 AMG V8 is the most expensive non‑V12 at £8,000–£14,000 supply.

How much does an AMG engine replacement cost?

An M177 AMG V8 recon costs £8,000–£14,000 supply; fitted, £9,000–£15,400. An M157 recon costs £6,000–£10,000 supply; fitted, £7,000–£11,400.

What is the 70% rule for Mercedes engine replacement?

The 70% rule: if the full fitted repair cost exceeds 70% of your Mercedes's current market value, the honest verdict is to scrap or sell for parts. Always apply this to your individual engine and vehicle.

How many Mercedes engine enquiries did you receive in 2025?

Mercedes accounted for over 5,000 of our 24,650 total engine enquiries in 2025, with OM651 alone generating 1,250 requests [EM-VERIFIED].
