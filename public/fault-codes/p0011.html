P0011 on a Mercedes means the ECU has detected that the intake camshaft on bank 1 (cylinder head 1) is over-advanced relative to the crankshaft position. On most M272, M273 and M276 engines this is a VVT solenoid, timing chain stretch or camshaft actuator problem - and it should be diagnosed cheapest-first.

  • Worst case: timing chain stretch requiring full chain and tensioner replacement, £1,800–£2,800 fitted [PATTERN DATA]

  • Frequently appears with P0012 or P0016 - the combination narrows the diagnosis considerably

What P0011 Actually Means

In a variable valve timing (VVT) system, the ECU commands the camshaft to advance or retard relative to the crankshaft to optimise performance and emissions. P0011 is set when the ECU commands the intake camshaft on bank 1 to move, but the actual camshaft position is over-advanced - meaning it has moved further than commanded or hasn't returned to the correct position. On Mercedes engines, this code is most commonly caused by a faulty VVT solenoid, low oil pressure, a worn timing chain, or a stuck camshaft adjuster. The code describes a position discrepancy, not a component failure - the distinction is the entire difference between a £120 solenoid and a £2,800 chain job.

Symptoms You'll Notice Alongside It

  • Engine management light illuminated, usually amber
  • Noticeable power loss or limp mode, especially under acceleration
  • Rough idle or misfire - see misfire & rough idle
  • Poor fuel economy - cam timing being off reduces efficiency
  • Cold-start rattle or hesitation on the first start of the day

Diagnostic Order - Cheapest First

1

Check oil level and condition

VVT is oil-pressure driven. Low oil level or a clogged filter can cause P0011. This is the cheapest possible fix.

2

Swap VVT solenoids between banks

A specialist swaps the bank 1 and bank 2 solenoids. If the code moves to bank 2 (P0021), the solenoid is at fault - simple replacement.

3

Live-data the camshaft position

Compare commanded vs actual camshaft position on live data. If actual follows command but is always offset, suspect chain stretch.

4

Check for timing chain stretch

Measure camshaft-to-crankshaft correlation at TDC. If the engine is out of time, it's almost always a stretched chain or worn guides.

5

Only then consider the camshaft adjuster

If steps 1–4 are clear and the code persists, the VVT phaser itself may be stuck or worn.

Can You Still Drive It?

Conditionally, yes - short journeys to get it diagnosed. P0011 on its own is not an engine-destroying fault the way an oil pressure warning is. But if the vehicle has dropped into limp mode, motorway driving becomes genuinely unsafe because you cannot accelerate normally. And if the cause is a stretched timing chain, prolonged driving risks the chain jumping a tooth - which can cause piston-to-valve contact and a full engine rebuild. Get it looked at within days, not weeks.

Related Fault Codes

P0011 rarely appears alone. Which codes accompany it materially changes the diagnosis.

P0012 - "A" Camshaft Position Timing Over-Retarded

Often triggered when the ECU can't advance the camshaft enough. If both P0011 and P0012 appear, suspect the VVT solenoid or low oil pressure.

P0016 - Crankshaft/Camshaft Correlation Bank 1

Strong evidence of timing chain stretch or a slipped sprocket. If P0016 is present, timing chain replacement is almost always required.

P0017 - Crankshaft/Camshaft Correlation Bank 2

If P0011 and P0017 appear together, suspect oil pressure issues or a fault affecting both banks.

P0300 - Random/Multiple Cylinder Misfire

Co-occurs with P0011 when cam timing is significantly off, causing misfires across multiple cylinders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drive with P0011 on a Mercedes?

For short journeys to reach a garage, generally yes - but not for normal use, and not on motorways if the vehicle has entered limp mode. Prolonged driving with a stretched timing chain risks catastrophic engine damage.

Does P0011 always mean the timing chain has failed?

No. The most common cause is a failing VVT solenoid, which costs around £120–£350 fitted. Timing chain stretch is less common and only becomes likely on higher-mileage pre-2013 M272/M273 engines.

Will clearing the code fix it?

Only temporarily. The code will return if the underlying fault persists. Clearing it destroys freeze-frame data a specialist would have used to narrow the cause.

How much should P0011 diagnosis cost?

Expect one to two hours of diagnostic time at an independent Mercedes specialist, typically offset against the repair if you proceed with them.
