What P0016 Actually Means

In a Mercedes V6 or V8 with variable valve timing, the ECU constantly compares the position of the crankshaft (which drives the pistons) with the intake camshaft on bank 1 (which controls the valves). P0016 is logged when the two are out of sync by more than a few degrees — the ECU knows exactly where the crank is and expects the cam to be within a tolerance window. If that window is exceeded, the code sets. Crucially, the code is a mechanical correlation error, not a sensor electrical fault in most cases. On M272/M273 engines, the primary cause is timing chain stretch — the chain elongates over time, throwing off the relationship between crank and cam. On M276, a failed tensioner or guide is more common. This code is a serious warning: if the chain jumps a tooth, pistons can hit valves, resulting in a full rebuild or replacement engine. That distinction is why early diagnosis is critical.

Symptoms You'll Notice Alongside It

  • Engine management light illuminated, often amber but may flash
  • Cold-start rattle lasting 1–2 seconds — a classic sign of chain or tensioner wear
  • Noticeable power loss or limp mode, especially under load
  • Rough idle or misfire — see misfire & rough idle
  • In severe cases, engine won't start or stalls unexpectedly

Diagnostic Order — Cheapest First

1

Check oil level and pressure

VVT relies on oil pressure. Low oil or a clogged filter can cause timing issues — this is the cheapest check.

2

Swap camshaft position sensors

Swap the bank 1 and bank 2 sensors. If the code moves to P0017 (bank 2), the sensor is faulty — cheap fix.

3

Live-data cam/crank correlation

A specialist will read the actual camshaft adaptation values. If the offset exceeds specification (typically >3–5°), chain stretch is confirmed.

4

Inspect timing chain and tensioner

If stretch is suspected, a physical inspection (removing cam covers) or using a timing chain stretch tool is required. Replacement is the definitive fix.

Can You Still Drive It?

Only to a garage, and only if absolutely necessary. P0016 indicates a mechanical timing error. If the chain is stretched, continuing to drive risks it skipping a tooth — which can cause piston-to-valve contact and a destroyed engine. If you hear a loud rattle on startup or the engine has gone into limp mode, stop immediately and arrange recovery. Do not attempt motorway journeys. The repair cost of a new engine (typically £5k–£10k) far exceeds the cost of a timing chain replacement (under £3k). Get it looked at within hours, not days.

Related Fault Codes

P0016 rarely appears alone. Which codes accompany it materially changes the diagnosis.

P0011 — "A" Camshaft Position Timing Over-Advanced

Often accompanies P0016 when the chain has stretched and the VVT system tries to compensate. If both appear, chain replacement is almost always required.

P0017 — Crankshaft/Camshaft Correlation Bank 2

If P0016 appears alone, it's bank 1 only. If both P0016 and P0017 appear, suspect overall chain stretch affecting both banks.

P0300 — Random/Multiple Cylinder Misfire

Co-occurs when timing errors cause misfires across cylinders, especially under load.

P0335 — Crankshaft Position Sensor Circuit

If P0335 appears with P0016, the crank sensor itself may be faulty, though correlation error is less likely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drive with P0016 on a Mercedes?

Only to a garage, and only if absolutely necessary. P0016 indicates a mechanical timing error. Driving risks chain skipping a tooth, causing catastrophic engine damage. Arrange recovery if the engine rattles or goes into limp mode.

Does P0016 always mean the timing chain needs replacing?

On M272/M273 engines, yes — chain stretch is the most common cause. On M276, a failed tensioner or guide is often the culprit. In either case, a full timing chain kit replacement is usually required.

Will clearing the code fix it?

No — the code will return as soon as the ECU rechecks correlation. Clearing it only removes freeze-frame data that a specialist could use to diagnose accurately.

How much should P0016 diagnosis cost?

Expect one to two hours of diagnostic time at an independent Mercedes specialist. Many offset this cost against the repair if you proceed with them.
