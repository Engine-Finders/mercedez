What Isn't Covered by a Recall

Recalls address safety-critical defects identified by the manufacturer or regulator. They do not cover general wear items, maintenance failures, or faults arising from previous owner neglect. Understanding this distinction prevents misdiagnosis and unnecessary expectation of free repair.

  • Active Body Control hydraulic pump wear on R230 - unless specifically cited in a recall with a revised part number, ABC pump degradation is considered normal wear and is not recalled. Our airmatic suspension compressor failure page explains the mechanism and typical repair costs [PATTERN DATA].
  • Battery parasitic drain on R230/R231 - this results from electronic module standby current draw exceeding specification, not a manufacturing defect. See our battery parasitic drain guide for diagnosis and cost bands [PATTERN DATA].
  • Retractable hardtop hydraulic pump failure on R230 - while documented in TSB LI77.10-P-051234, this is classified as a service item, not a safety recall. Repair costs typically range £800–£2,200 depending on pump and seal condition [EM-QUOTE].
  • Wiring loom corrosion on R129 - age-related deterioration of insulation is not recalled. Our wiring loom corrosion page covers identification and remediation [PATTERN DATA].

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if my Mercedes SL has an outstanding recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN into the checker above or visit the official GOV.UK vehicle recall service. Both sources query the same DVSA database and return results instantly. You can also contact any Mercedes-Benz retailer with your VIN - they will confirm status and book the repair if needed.

Are recall repairs really free, even on older SL models like the R129?

Yes. Safety recalls are mandated by law and must be completed at no cost to the vehicle owner, regardless of age, mileage, or number of previous keepers. This includes parts, labour, and any associated diagnostics. Authorised dealers and many independent specialists in our vetted network can perform recall work.

What's the difference between a recall and a Technical Service Bulletin?

A recall addresses a safety defect and is legally enforceable - the manufacturer must notify owners and fix it free of charge. A TSB documents a known non-safety fault for workshop reference; repairs are only free if the vehicle is under warranty or the manufacturer offers goodwill coverage. TSBs are valuable for diagnosis but do not carry the same legal obligation.

My SL isn't listed here - does that mean it has no recalls?

Not necessarily. This page covers the most significant campaigns for R129, R230, R231, and R232 generations. Earlier models and niche variants may have separate campaigns. Always verify via VIN check rather than relying solely on published lists. If your chassis code isn't shown, use the SL hub to navigate to your specific generation.
