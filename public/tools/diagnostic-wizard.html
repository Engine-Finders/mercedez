How to Read Your Diagnostic Results

Each result the wizard returns contains four pieces of information. Understanding what they mean helps you decide the next step.

Likelihood Ranking

Causes are ordered by how frequently our specialist network encounters them for your specific symptom, engine, and mileage combination. Rank 1 is the most statistically likely cause — not a certainty.

Severity Rating

Each cause carries a fixed severity indicator using our universal scale: Catastrophic (engine at risk), Immediate (needs attention within days), Monitor (watch and re-check), or Low (unlikely to worsen quickly).

Cost Band

The typical UK independent-specialist repair cost range for that specific fix, drawn from actual supplier quotes processed through our network [EM-QUOTE]. Main dealer rates are typically 30–50% higher [PATTERN DATA].

Next Step Link

Every result links to either a full failure explanation page, a fault code page, or a symptom hub where you can read the mechanism, see what a specialist will check, and understand whether the repair is financially sensible for your vehicle's value.

If your result shows a knocking noise with OM651 at high mileage, for instance, the top-ranked cause will typically be balance shaft or timing chain wear — and the wizard will tell you that directly, with the cost band attached. If it shows misfire or rough idle on an M274, the ranking shifts toward injector or coil pack issues. The context changes everything.

Where a symptom could indicate something urgent — overheating or coolant loss with no visible leak — the wizard flags this explicitly and advises you not to drive the car until a specialist has looked at it. It will always err on the side of caution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Mercedes diagnostic wizard actually free?

Yes, completely. No email address, no phone number, no account. You select your symptoms, answer the narrowing questions, and receive your ranked result immediately. If you then want a repair quote, we route you to our specialist network — but the diagnosis itself costs nothing and carries no obligation.

Can this replace a proper OBD code read?

No. The wizard works at the symptom level — before any code reader is plugged in. It helps you understand what's likely wrong and what it might cost, so you walk into a workshop conversation informed. A specialist will still plug in a diagnostic tool, read stored fault codes, and confirm the finding. Think of this as the first five minutes of that process, done from your kitchen table.

Which Mercedes models does the wizard cover?

All major Mercedes engine families in our dataset: OM651 and OM654 diesels (C-Class, E-Class, GLC, Sprinter), OM642 V6 diesel (E-Class, GLE, ML), M276 V6 petrol, M274 four-cylinder turbo petrol, and AMG variants where we hold sufficient data [EM-VERIFIED]. If your engine isn't covered, the wizard tells you so and routes you to our general symptom hub instead.

What happens after I get my result?

Three options: read the full explanation page linked in your result; use our repair vs replace calculator to check whether the repair makes financial sense for your car's value; or request quotes from our vetted Mercedes specialist network. The wizard never pressures you toward any single option.
