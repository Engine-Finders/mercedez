What the Results Mean

Supply value is not fitted cost. Every figure this tool returns is supply-only — the price of the engine itself, delivered. Fitting adds removal and refit labour, which runs 8–12 hours for the four-cylinder OM651 and OM654 and 10–16 hours for the V6 and V8 units [EM-QUOTE]. At the £65/hr independent baseline used in our data (North England/Midlands), with London and the South East adding 40–60% [EM-QUOTE], that labour component is often a third of the total bill. Our supply and fit explainer breaks the two apart in full.

The three condition tiers answer different questions. A used engine is the cheapest route but carries unknown history; a reconditioned unit has been stripped, inspected and rebuilt to a standard; a rebuilt engine is the most thorough and the most expensive. Which makes sense for your car depends on the vehicle's value — our condition comparison guide sets out the trade-offs.

Value is only half the decision. What matters is the repair cost measured against your car's worth. For Mercedes we apply a 70% repair-to-value threshold: once the cheapest viable replacement approaches 70% of the vehicle's market value, scrapping or part-exchanging deserves serious consideration. The full logic, with worked examples, lives in our repair, replace or scrap framework, and engine-by-engine figures are listed in the replacement cost by engine code table.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Mercedes engine value checker free?

Yes — completely free, and no email address is required to see your result. Email capture, where offered, only ever appears after your result has been shown, never as a gate.

How accurate are the value ranges?

The ranges come from real supplier quotes routed through our platform in 2025, cross-checked against marketplace listings [EM-QUOTE]. They are bands, not fixed prices: condition, mileage, warranty and regional availability all move the final figure, so treat the midpoint as a realistic expectation and confirm with quotes.

What's the difference between this and the repair vs replace calculator?

This tool tells you what an engine is worth on the supply market. The repair vs replace calculator takes that value, adds labour, and weighs the total against your specific vehicle's worth using the 70% Mercedes threshold. Most owners use this tool first, then the calculator.

What if my engine code isn't listed?

Confirm the code using the engine code guide or the physical stamp on the block, then check back — coverage expands regularly. For older OM646 or OM651-era units, the OM651 knowledge centre and its siblings cover the full family history and fitment.
