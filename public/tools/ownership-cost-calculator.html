What Your Results Mean

Four figures, each answering a different question about the cost of owning your Mercedes.

Fitted replacement cost — what a reconditioned engine would cost installed, combining supply cost, R&R labour at the £65/hour independent baseline and the typical fitted premium [EM-QUOTE]. Labour rates run 40–60% higher in London and the South East [EM-QUOTE]; our labour rates and fitting times guide breaks this down region by region.

Repair-to-value ratio — the fitted cost measured against your car's current market value. Mercedes ownership sits under a 70% threshold: once a replacement approaches or exceeds 70% of the car's value, evaluate scrap or part-exchange before committing. The full logic, including a worked example, is published in our repair, replace or scrap framework.

Verdict flag — Safe, Watch or Scrap Threshold, mirroring the flags used across our model decision tables. If yours shows Watch or Scrap Threshold, run the figures through our repair vs replace calculator before deciding anything.

Annual provision — the fitted cost spread across the years you plan to keep the car. Set this aside each year alongside your servicing budget so a failure is never a financial shock. Fuel, insurance, tax and tyres are excluded: this is a Mercedes engine ownership cost calculator, not a whole-life cost sheet.

Where Our Cost Data Comes From

Every cost in this tool is drawn from our 2025 proprietary dataset: 24,650 real UK quote requests processed through EnginesMarket.co.uk [EM-VERIFIED], with supply and fitted pricing taken from actual supplier quotes [EM-QUOTE]. Typical market value bands come from Auto Trader and Motors.co.uk listings data [THIRD-PARTY].

Two honest limitations. First, our enquiry data reflects owners seeking replacements, not a random sample of every Mercedes on UK roads — request volume is not failure rate. Second, your individual car's condition, history and specification can move every figure shown here. The full methodology, including exactly what counts as an enquiry, is published on our data methodology page, and every source tier is explained on our data verification page.

The tool currently covers the five engines that dominate our enquiry data: the OM651 — the most-enquired Mercedes engine in our dataset, with 1,250 requests in 2025 [EM-VERIFIED] — the OM642 V6 at 880 requests [EM-VERIFIED], plus the OM654, M276 and M177. More engines are added as quote volumes allow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Mercedes ownership cost calculator free to use?

Completely free, with no email address required to see your result. Enter your model, engine and vehicle value and the estimate appears instantly. Email capture is offered only after your result, and only so we can route quotes from our vetted specialist network if you choose to request them.

How accurate are the calculator's UK cost estimates?

Cost bands come from real supplier quotes processed during 2025 [EM-QUOTE], so they reflect actual UK market pricing rather than list prices. Your individual vehicle's condition, mileage and location will shift the numbers — London and South East labour alone adds 40–60% [EM-QUOTE] — so treat the result as a budgeting guide and confirm any major decision with a specialist.

Does the estimate include fuel, insurance and road tax?

No. This tool measures engine ownership exposure: replacement cost, repair-to-value position and an annual repair provision. Fuel, insurance, tax, tyres and servicing sit outside it. Add your own known servicing costs to the annual provision figure for a complete picture of your Mercedes annual running costs.

Which engines does the Mercedes engine ownership cost calculator cover?

Currently the five engines that dominate our enquiry data: OM651, OM642 and OM654 diesels, plus M276 and M177 petrol units. If your engine isn't listed — an older OM646 or an AMG M156, for example — our replacement cost by engine code guide covers the full range.
