What Your Mercedes Engine Lookup Results Mean

The engine code is your vehicle's confirmed primary unit. For badges that span two different engines across their production run — some C-Class and E-Class diesels, for example — the lookup flags this and points you to the engine number location guide to confirm which you actually have before you commit to a replacement.

The verdict uses the same 70% repair-to-value framework published openly on our repair, replace or scrap framework page. It's a starting guide based on typical values for your model, not a substitute for your own vehicle's specific valuation — a low-mileage or well-specced C-Class W205 can justify a replacement that a tired example can't. Every result ends with a route to a quote from our vetted specialist network, so you can act on the verdict immediately.

Where Our Mercedes Registration Data Comes From

The fault and cost data behind this tool is drawn from the same 2025 UK enquiry dataset behind every other page on this site — 24,650 quote requests handled across the full year [EM-VERIFIED]. Mercedes-specific volumes include 1,250 OM651 enquiries, 880 OM642 and 520 OM654 [EM-VERIFIED], which is what lets the lookup pair each engine with its most common failures and realistic replacement costs. Full methodology and limitations are set out on our Data Methodology page, and every figure is source-checked per our data verification standards.

Build and fitment data is cross-referenced against manufacturer records and independent parts suppliers, so the tool distinguishes an OM651 from an OM654 even where the badge alone is ambiguous.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Mercedes registration lookup free?

Yes — a completely free Mercedes registration lookup online, with no email required to see your result. You only provide details if you choose to request a quote afterwards.

How accurate is the engine identification?

Very high for the primary engine fitted to your model and generation. For badges known to span two engines across their production run, we show both possibilities and recommend confirming against your engine number before ordering parts.

What if my registration isn't recognised?

Use the full fitment matrix to identify your engine manually by model, generation and build year, or read the engine number stamp directly with our engine number location guide.

Does the lookup work for every Mercedes model?

It covers the engines we hold UK enquiry data for — A-Class through S-Class, plus GLA, GLC, GLE and the Sprinter and Vito vans. If your vehicle uses a rarer unit, the fitment matrix will still place it.
