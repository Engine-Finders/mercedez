Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Mercedes repair vs replace calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no email required and no data stored. You enter two figures, the calculator applies the 70% threshold, and your verdict appears instantly. If you want to proceed to a live quote comparison afterwards, that is also free and handled by our vetted specialist network.

What does the 70% threshold mean?

If your quoted repair or replacement cost exceeds 70% of your vehicle's current market value, the calculator flags it as Watch or Scrap Threshold. This figure reflects the parts, labour and residual-value baselines we observe specifically in Mercedes replacement enquiries [EM-QUOTE]. It is higher than the 60% threshold used for Land Rover and Jaguar, reflecting genuinely different cost structures.

How do I know my Mercedes' current value?

Check recent sold listings on AutoTrader or Motors.co.uk for your exact model, year, mileage and spec. Use the private-sale figure, not the dealer retail price, and not the trade-in offer you might receive. Our engine value checker can help contextualise the result against typical replacement costs for your engine code.

What if my result shows Scrap Threshold?

It does not automatically mean you should scrap the car. Rare specs, low mileage or sentimental value may justify replacement above the threshold. But it does mean you should get a scrap or part-exchange valuation before committing to a repair quote. Our write-off categories guide explains how insurers and buyers assess these situations.
