What a Mercedes VIN Can and Can't Tell You

Every Mercedes-Benz VIN is 17 characters long and follows the international ISO 3779 standard, but Mercedes encodes specific information in predictable positions. Understanding the structure helps you interpret your result and know what to confirm separately.

Position Contains Example
1–3 World Manufacturer Identifier (WMI) WDD = Mercedes-Benz Germany, W1K = Mercedes-Benz passenger cars [PATTERN DATA]
4–8 Vehicle Descriptor Section (model series, body type, restraint system) Identifies W205, W213, W167 etc. [PATTERN DATA]
9 Check digit (mathematical validation) Used to verify VIN authenticity [PATTERN DATA]
10 Model year Letter or digit encoding the model year [PATTERN DATA]
11 Assembly plant Sindelfingen, Bremen, Rastatt, Tuscaloosa etc. [PATTERN DATA]
12–17 Production sequence number Unique serial for your specific vehicle [PATTERN DATA]

What the VIN cannot tell you: Unlike some manufacturers, Mercedes-Benz does not encode the exact engine code directly in the VIN. The engine is specified on the vehicle's data card (build sheet) using option codes. Our decoder cross-references the model series and production period against our fitment database to show the most probable engine family, but for absolute confirmation - particularly when sourcing a replacement engine - you should verify using the engine number stamp or request the data card from a Mercedes-Benz dealer [PATTERN DATA].

This distinction matters when ordering parts or a replacement engine. The difference between an OM651 and an OM654 in the same C-Class badge is significant for compatibility - see our OM vs M Series identification guide for how to confirm which you have.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Mercedes VIN decoder free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result. The tool processes your VIN instantly and shows the full decode without any signup or payment gate.

Can a Mercedes VIN tell me the exact engine code?

Not directly. Mercedes-Benz encodes the model series and body type in the VIN but not the specific engine code. The engine is identified via the data card (build sheet). Our decoder shows the most probable engine family based on model and production period, then directs you to confirm via the engine number stamp.

Where do I find my Mercedes VIN?

The VIN appears in three locations: on a metal plate at the base of the windscreen (driver's side), on a sticker in the driver's door jamb, and on your V5C registration document. All three should match exactly.

What if my VIN isn't recognised?

If the decoder cannot parse your VIN, check that all 17 characters are entered correctly with no spaces. If it still fails, use our Fitment Matrix to identify your engine manually by model and build year, or check the engine number stamp directly using our Engine Number Location guide.
